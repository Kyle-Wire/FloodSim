        Runoff and soils Advanced

        Sediment, debris flows and banks Advanced

        Four grain classes from the soil survey (clay, silt, sand, gravel): erosion by excess shear, settling by size, sand and gravel bedload (Wilcock & Crowe), and the ground moving with it. Standard solver only (not the subgrid speed mode).

        The flow becomes a water–sediment mixture that bulks as it picks up the bed, entrains on slopes over 10° and drops its load where it flattens (Egashira), and slows with a yield stress and viscosity that rise with concentration (O'Brien & Julien). Steep soil saturated by the storm fails as a shallow landslide (infinite slope, Green-Ampt wetting front) and joins the flow. On slopes over 10° the whole soil to bedrock is erodible.

        Every step, for the ground within 20 m of open water: a planar (Culmann) failure through the toe with the soil's strength and roots, the river confining the bank, the bank's water table lagging the river (rapid drawdown), and toe scour when sediment transport is on. The probability comes from the spread of the soil's strength. Standard solver only.

        Storm settings lock once a run starts; Reset to change them. Roof rain goes to the open cells around each building, except the share whose downspouts connect to the sewer (Drainage).

        Combined sewer Sanitary, regulators, gates

        Each outfall to a stream becomes a regulator: flow up to the interceptor's capacity goes on to treatment, the rest spills over a weir to the river (a combined sewer overflow). People per manhole from the land cover it serves (8 / 25 / 60 / 120 per ha of developed open / low / medium / high intensity), on a typical residential daily pattern. Pipes leaving the area carry on to the interceptor as before.

        Green infrastructure and storage Area-wide

        Pavement stores 200 mm and takes water at 1000 mm/hr; rain gardens store 300 mm at 100 mm/hr; both drain to the native soil at its SSURGO rate. Green roofs hold the first 25 mm of rain. Tanks are SWMM storage units at the manholes feeding each outfall (pipe network only).
