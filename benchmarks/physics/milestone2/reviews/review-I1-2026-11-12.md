# Technical review I1: PVM2 / PV-TF1 draft 2 (received 12 Nov 2026)

Filed as received, in substance. The response is in `resolution-I1.md`.

**Verdict:** not approved to run. Two **blocking** objections must be resolved before PV-TF1 returns for review.

## Blocking objection 1: the full-conduit exclusion guard

The current F1 exclusion guard checks endpoint node depths against 0.9 D. That is insufficient, because the failure
mechanism under investigation concerns SWMM's full-conduit / crown-cutoff behaviour.

Required:
- Retain the existing endpoint-node depth guard.
- Add a guard that checks the maximum water depth in every conduit segment at every routing time step.
- If any segment reaches or exceeds 0.9 D at any point, invalidate the experiment for scoring.
- Record the maximum observed depth, the corresponding conduit or segment, the time step, and the depth-to-diameter
  ratio.
- Make the exclusion decision from the full time series, not just the final or resting state.
- Use the actual link-depth variables available from the simulation. Do not infer conduit depth from endpoint node
  depths.

## Blocking objection 2: rest-state hydraulic equilibrium

The current rest criteria establish stable, equal tank levels, low segment flows and zero weir flow. They do not
establish that the entire pipe system has reached the uniform hydraulic state assumed by reference R1.

Required:
- During every time step of the declared rest window, inspect every intermediate junction and every conduit segment.
- Verify that their water-surface elevations agree with the common tank water-surface elevation within 1 mm.
- Compare elevations in a common vertical datum; do not compare raw depths where invert elevations differ.
- Retain the existing flow and weir-flow criteria.
- If any internal location violates the elevation tolerance, classify the run as Insufficient reference data, not as a
  pass or a fail.
- Record the maximum internal elevation deviation, its location and the time step at which it occurs.

## Integrity

- Do not relax, widen or replace the existing closure or level criteria.
- Do not alter the 180.0 m³ inflow, the geometry, the boundary conditions or the competing predictions.
- Keep the P1/P2a/P2b/P3 structure.
- No change to production code or to SWMM behaviour.
- No run, no hash, no registration and no freeze.
- Make the smallest necessary changes.
- Do not resolve any ambiguity by an undocumented assumption. If the available output cannot support a required check,
  stop and explain the limitation.
