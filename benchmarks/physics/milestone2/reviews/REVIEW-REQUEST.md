# Request for independent review: PVM2 and PV-TF1 (draft 2)

**What is under review:**
- the scope of Physics Verification Milestone 2 (PVM2);
- its candidate list;
- the full registration of its proposed first experiment, PV-TF1.

Nothing has been run. You may reject PV-TF1, any candidate or the scope **without agreeing to its implementation and
without proposing one.** A rejection with reasons is a complete review.

## Read, in this order

1. `../../../FINDINGS.md`: the findings F1–F9, with the evidence and the review 5 resolutions at the end.
2. `../REGISTRATION.md`: the scope, the candidates and the references. Its "Revisions" section at the end governs where
   it differs from the text above it.
3. `../spec-proposed/PV-TF1.json`: the experiment as it would be run. This is the authoritative definition.
4. `review-5-2026-11-12.md` and `resolution-5.md`: what changed from draft 1 and why.
5. Background, if needed:
   - `../../README.md` (the suite);
   - `../../spec/PV-C1.json` (the closed-system benchmark whose criteria PV-TF1 carries);
   - `../../../culvert/README.md` (T2-C, B6-C);
   - `../../spec-revisions/PV-U1-definition-revision-1.md` (F2).

SWMM source references point to `benchmarks/culvert/instrumented/build/src/`, an unmodified copy of SWMM 5.2.4. It is
generated by `../../../culvert/instrumented/build.mjs` and not in git.

## Please scrutinise specifically

1. **The hydrostatic reference and its assumptions (R1–R6).**
   - Is the at-rest state truly independent of SWMM?
   - Is L·A(h) exact for this geometry?
   - Is anything stored that the reference omits?
2. **Internal-junction storage and segmentation (J1–J3).**
   - R4 (junction volume ≡ 0) matches SWMM by construction. Does that make the junction-volume row uninformative?
   - Should a manhole with a real plan area be its own case?
   - Is the J2 bound right? Is J2 observable enough to support P2b?
3. **The definition of rest.**
   - Are equal, stable tank levels plus a per-segment flow ≤ 5×10⁻⁶ m³/s plus zero boundary flux sufficient?
   - Are they reachable within 12 h in a 400 m horizontal conduit, which may oscillate slowly?
   - Is "not at rest → Insufficient reference data" the right consequence?
4. **Can the experiment distinguish the predictions?** P1 (the open outfall), P2a (link storage; falling with N),
   P2b (junction minimum area; growing with N), P3 (F1 excluded).
   - Are the opposite N trends and the MIN_SURFAREA diagnostic enough?
   - Which outcomes would remain ambiguous?
5. **Are the interpretations limited enough?** Check each row of `outcomeInterpretation`, in particular that passing at
   rest is not read as transient correctness (`transientStatement`), and that no outcome closes T2-C, B6-C or F4.
6. **The relationship to F1–F5 and the culvert findings** (`relationToFindings`).
   - Is F1 really excluded by the 0.9 D guard?
   - Does anything here silently re-baseline T2-C or PV-X1?
7. **The criteria.**
   - They are PV-C1's, plus conditions added in draft 2.
   - Are they justified independently of what the experiment is expected to show?
   - Is any one set to make a pass easier?

## Reply

Please give:
- a verdict: **approve**, **approve with required changes**, or **reject**;
- numbered objections, each marked **blocking** or **non-blocking**.

Your review is filed unedited as `review-I<n>-<date>.md`, and each objection gets a recorded response in
`resolution-I<n>.md`.

**PV-TF1 is approved only when no blocking objection remains open across all three reviews.**
