# Per-jurisdiction design-criteria matrix

Every engineering decision FloodSim makes on the user's behalf, with the two jurisdictions' values beside it.

**Editions stamped:** PWD SMGM **v3.4** · PennDOT Pub 584 **2015 Ed. Change #1 (June 2022)** · PennDOT Pub 13M
(DM-2) **2015 Ed., Chapter 10 pages Change #9, Chapter 13 pages Change #8** · Philadelphia Code Title 4 /
Title 14 as of 1 November 2026. Compiled 1 November 2026. Re-check on any republication.

**Verdict** is what a reviewer in that jurisdiction would most likely do with the default *as shipped*:

- **Accept** — meets or exceeds the criterion, or the criterion does not apply.
- **Question** — defensible but not the jurisdiction's stated value or method; expect a comment, and expect to
  have to justify it in the narrative.
- **Reject** — contradicts a mandatory criterion. The submittal does not pass with this default in place.
- **n/a** — the jurisdiction publishes no criterion. *Not* a pass: it means the number is ours to defend, or
  belongs to a third reviewer (e.g. Philadelphia Streets Department for street geometry).

**Fix class** — `param` configurable parameter · `preset` per-jurisdiction preset · `gap` genuine capability
gap · `label` wording/labelling only (the number is fine, the claim about it is not).
